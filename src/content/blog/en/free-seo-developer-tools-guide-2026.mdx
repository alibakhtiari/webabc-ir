## 2. Deep Dive: Essential Free Tools for Marketers & Engineers

### 1. [SEO Title Checker](/en/tools/seo-title-checker) & Pixel Verification

Never guess whether Google will truncate your meta title. The [SEO Title Checker](/en/tools/seo-title-checker) measures exact pixel dimensions across desktop and mobile viewports, alerting you before titles get cut off with ellipses.

### 2. [Headline Analyzer](/en/tools/headline-analyzer) for Maximum CTR

Organic rankings mean nothing if searchers don't click. Our [Headline Analyzer](/en/tools/headline-analyzer) evaluates word balance, power phrases, emotional impact, and optimal character lengths to turn standard titles into high-converting click magnets.

### 3. [SERP Preview Tool](/en/tools/serp-preview)

Visualize how your title, URL breadcrumb, meta description, and rich snippet stars will render across Google mobile and desktop result cards with the [SERP Preview](/en/tools/serp-preview) utility.

### 4. [JSON-LD Schema Generator](/en/tools/schema-generator)

Schema.org markup is the primary language of Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and AI engines like ChatGPT Search and Perplexity. Create compliant `Article`, `FAQPage`, `LocalBusiness`, and `Product` schemas instantly with the [Schema Generator](/en/tools/schema-generator).

### 5. [Keyword Density Analyzer](/en/tools/keyword-density-analyzer)

Audit keyword distribution to prevent unintentional keyword stuffing while ensuring core topical semantic coverage with the [Keyword Density Analyzer](/en/tools/keyword-density-analyzer).

### 6. [UTM Campaign Builder](/en/tools/utm-builder)

Ensure your marketing attribution stays pristine across email, social, and PPC channels with our standardized [UTM Builder](/en/tools/utm-builder).
