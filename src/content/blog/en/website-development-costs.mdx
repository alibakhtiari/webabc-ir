Determining how much a website will cost depends on complexity, features, and platform choices.

## Factors Driving Costs

### 1. Platform Choice
- **WordPress**: Economical and quick setup for standard business portfolios.
- **Custom Coding (Next.js/Astro)**: Premium, ultra-fast, and secure custom applications.

### 2. Page Count
Every unique page layout design adds to design mockup and coding hours.

### 3. Special Features
Addons like multi-language support (RTL/LTR), custom database systems, or secure member portals scale development time.
