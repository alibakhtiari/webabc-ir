For companies targeting regional markets in Dubai, Muscat, or Tehran, local SEO is the fastest path to attracting high-intent clients.

## Key Local SEO Steps

- **Optimize Google Maps**: Ensure exact matching of name, address, and phone number.
- **Multilingual SEO**: Structure your pages to cater to Arabic, Farsi, and English searches.
- **Local Schemas**: Use `LocalBusiness` structured JSON-LD schemas in the HTML header.
