Modern web design in 2026 focuses heavily on performance combined with visual excellence.

## Visual Design Trends

- **Glassmorphism**: Elegant semi-transparent cards using backdrop filters. Try our free [Glassmorphism Generator](/en/tools/glassmorphism-generator/).
- **Micro-animations**: Subtle interactions indicating clickable or hover states.
- **Adaptive Dark Mode**: Automatic theme switching for optimal night reading.
- **Color Contrast & Accessibility**: High contrast ratios for maximum readability. Test your palette with our free [Color Contrast Checker](/en/tools/color-contrast-checker/).
