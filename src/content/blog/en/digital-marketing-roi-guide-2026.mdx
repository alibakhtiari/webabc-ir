## 2. Measuring Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) & Lifetime Value (LTV)

Evaluating campaign efficiency relies on tracking key financial ratios:

- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: Maintaining a healthy 3:1 ratio (Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost) for sustainable business growth.
- **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)**: Testing headline variations and page calls-to-action to lower per-lead costs.
- **Attribution Modeling**: Moving beyond last-click attribution to understand full multi-touch customer journeys.
