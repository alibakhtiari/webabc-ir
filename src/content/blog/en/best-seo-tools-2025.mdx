SEO tools are critical to understanding how search engines view and rank your site. Selecting the right stack will streamline keyword research, competitor analysis, and site auditing.

## Key Tool Categories

### 1. Analytics & Performance Tracking
- **Google Search Console**: Essential for tracking keyword impressions and clicks.
- **Google Analytics 4**: Measures on-site user behavior.

### 2. On-Page SEO Optimizers
- **Headline Analyzer**: Helps draft high-converting, search-friendly titles.
- **Keyword Density Tool**: Prevents keyword stuffing and improves word placement.
