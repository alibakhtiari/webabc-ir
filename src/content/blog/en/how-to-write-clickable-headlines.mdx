Your headline is the first thing users see on Google. An optimized, high-CTR title makes a massive difference in attracting organic clicks.

## Tips for Better Headlines

- **Ideal Length**: 50 to 60 characters is the sweet spot.
- **Add Numbers**: Headlines with numbers get up to 36% higher CTRs.
- **Use Emotional Triggers**: Words like "Proven," "Easy," or "Secrets" spark curiosity.
