## 1. Project Overview

**Tahami Aesthetic & Surgery Clinic** (tahamiclinic.ir) is a premier cosmetic healthcare institution situated in the upscale medical district of Northern Tehran on Nelson Mandela Boulevard (Jordan). Staffed by certified plastic surgeons, dermatologists, and aesthetic specialists, Tahami Clinic offers a comprehensive spectrum of high-ticket surgical and minimally invasive aesthetic treatments, including upper and lower blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery), submental double chin liposuction, advanced facial contouring, natural hair and eyebrow transplants, subcision for deep acne scar revision, and medical-grade skin rejuvenation.

While Tahami Clinic possessed world-class surgical facilities, advanced clinical technologies, and exceptional patient outcomes, its digital presence struggled to capture the enormous volume of high-intent patient searches originating across Tehran. To overcome aggressive regional competition and establish uncontested search leadership, the clinic partnered with WebABC to deploy an enterprise-grade medical search strategy through our specialized [SEO services](/en/services/seo/) and localized [Tehran digital marketing solutions](/en/service-areas/tehran/).

## 2. Key Challenges

- **Stringent Google YMYL & Healthcare Search Guidelines:** Aesthetic surgery falls strictly under Google's "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) content classification. Algorithmic quality filters enforce uncompromising E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) standards, penalizing medical portals that lack accredited physician authorship, verifiable clinical qualifications, and transparent safety disclosures.
- **Fierce Regional Aesthetic Competition:** Northern Tehran—particularly the Jordan, Elahiyeh, Zaferaniyeh, and Sa'adat Abad corridors—represents one of the most competitive aesthetic medicine markets in the Middle East. Legacy clinics and established private practices heavily contend for top Google positions and Google Maps 3-Pack visibility.
- **High-Ticket Patient Trust & Frictionless Conversion:** Cosmetic surgeries represent high-consideration personal investments. Prospective patients demand comprehensive procedural clarity, realistic before-and-after documentation, verified surgeon credentials, and seamless pathways to connect directly with patient coordinators via phone inquiries (`021-71031`) and online consultation forms.
- **Technical SEO Gaps & Mobile Speed Bottlenecks:** The clinic’s previous web platform suffered from unoptimized high-resolution imagery, missing structured clinical schema markup, and sluggish mobile page load times that degraded both search rankings and mobile conversion rates.

## 3. Implemented Solutions

### Medical E-E-A-T Architecture & High-Intent Clinical Content

To satisfy Google's clinical search algorithms and educate prospective patients, WebABC re-architected the website's content hierarchy around high-ticket aesthetic procedures:

- **Specialized Procedural Hubs:** Developed in-depth, medically reviewed pillar guides for core surgical services: blepharoplasty, double chin liposuction, facial contouring, natural hair and eyebrow restoration, and subcision.
- **Physician Accreditation & Transparency:** Created dedicated, schema-verified doctor profiles detailing medical board certifications, surgical specializations, clinical experience, and published medical contributions.
- **Patient Safety & Care Guides:** Embedded comprehensive pre-operative guidelines, post-operative recovery timelines, and candid risk disclosures to foster patient trust and maximize algorithmic E-E-A-T evaluation.

### Local Search Dominance & Google Business Profile Optimization

Leveraging the methodologies detailed in our comprehensive [Local SEO Services Guide 2026](/en/blog/local-seo-services-guide-2026/), WebABC executed an aggressive localized search campaign:

- **Google Business Profile (GBP) Mastery:** Completely optimized Tahami Clinic's GBP listing with primary categories (*Plastic Surgery Clinic*, *Medical Clinic*), detailed service menus with transparent starting pricing brackets, and regular weekly geotagged clinic photography.
- **Cross-Platform Map Synchronization:** Synchronized business name, address, and telephone (NAP) data across Google Maps, Apple Maps, and prominent regional navigation directories (Neshan and Balad) to build unbreakable algorithmic entity trust.
- **Review Sentiment & Acceleration:** Established an automated, post-consultation feedback collection system that encouraged satisfied patients to leave detailed, keyword-rich reviews mentioning specific procedures (*"blepharoplasty in Jordan"*, *"double chin liposuction"*).

### Structured Medical Schema.org Engineering

To ensure deep semantic understanding by search crawlers, voice assistants, and AI answer engines, we engineered customized, interconnected JSON-LD schema markup:

- **`MedicalBusiness` & `PlasticSurgeryClinic`:** Defined the clinic's physical coordinates, opening hours, accepted payment types, and geographical service radius across Tehran.
- **`Physician` Entity Schemas:** Linked surgical procedures directly to board-certified attending specialists via `alumniOf`, `medicalSpecialty`, and `knowsAbout` properties.
- **`MedicalProcedure` & `FAQPage` Markup:** Embedded rich procedure-level schemas detailing surgical methods, preparation steps, and direct answers to common patient concerns for immediate extraction in Google AI Overviews and featured snippets.

### High-Conversion Patient Acquisition Funnels

Aesthetic web traffic must translate efficiently into clinical consultations. WebABC overhauled the user experience to eliminate booking friction:

- **Direct Call Routing:** Integrated high-visibility click-to-call buttons connected to the clinic's primary PBX hotline (`021-71031`), ensuring mobile searchers can instantly reach patient coordinators.
- **Multi-Channel Consultation Options:** Implemented low-friction WhatsApp consultation bridges and streamlined appointment request forms with automated SMS confirmations.
- **Trust Elements & Proof Points:** Positioned accredited surgeon credentials, verified patient testimonials, and high-definition clinical facility photography at primary decision points across all landing pages.

### Performance Engineering & Core Web Vitals Hardening

Working in synergy with our [local SEO services](/en/services/local-seo/), we refactored the underlying WordPress architecture:

- Optimized image delivery pipelines utilizing next-gen WebP/AVIF compression to ensure high-resolution clinical photos load instantly on 4G/5G mobile networks.
- Eliminated render-blocking CSS and deferred non-critical JavaScript, lifting mobile Core Web Vitals to green across LCP, INP, and CLS.
- Elevated the platform's technical SEO health audit score to an outstanding **98/100**.
