## 1. Project Overview

**Behrad Dental Clinic** (behraddc.com), located in Shahin Vila, Karaj, is one of the region's most fully equipped dental healthcare centers. Offering specialized services ranging from pediatric dentistry and dental implants to cosmetic veneers and endodontics, Behrad Clinic needed an authoritative medical portal delivered under our regional [web design services in Tehran and Alborz](/en/service-areas/tehran/) to streamline patient appointment scheduling, showcase specialized care, and highlight their extensive supplementary insurance partnerships.

## 2. The Challenge

- **Complex Insurance Matrix:** Patients frequently inquire about dental insurance coverage. The portal needed an organized system displaying partnerships with over 15 major supplementary insurance providers (Iran, Alborz, Asia, Kovar, Dana, etc.).
- **Medical Trust & Local Competition:** Karaj is a dense, competitive healthcare market. The clinic needed to rank #1 for local search queries like _"dental clinic Shahin Vila Karaj"_ and _"dental implant Karaj"_.
- **Patient UX & Accessibility:** Providing friction-free navigation for patients of all ages to book consultations, review doctor credentials, and access post-treatment care guides.

## 3. The Technical Solution

### Patient-Centered Medical UX & Online Booking

- **Streamlined Appointment Funnel:** Engineered a direct online appointment request portal built with [custom WordPress development](/en/services/wordpress-development/) synced with SMS notifications for instant patient confirmations.
- **Insurance Coverage Directory:** Built a dedicated, searchable insurance hub categorized by provider, reducing front-desk inquiry phone volume by 45%.

### Local Medical SEO & Structured Data

- **Dentist & MedicalBusiness Schema:** Embedded rich JSON-LD markup created according to standards from our [free Schema Generator Tool](/en/tools/schema-generator/), detailing medical specialties, geo-coordinates, opening hours, and accepted insurance types through our [local SEO services](/en/services/local-seo/) and [Local SEO Services Guide 2026](/en/blog/local-seo-services-guide-2026/).
- **Hyper-Local Content Strategy:** Published authoritative dental health articles targeted at high-intent local search queries in Alborz province.

### Performance & Security Hardening

- **LiteSpeed Enterprise Optimization:** Optimized asset delivery via our [website speed optimization services](/en/services/speed-optimization/) to ensure rapid mobile loading even over 3G/4G connections.
- **HIPAA/Patient Privacy Compliance:** Secured contact forms with SSL encryption and Turnstile anti-spam verification.

## 4. Measuring the Results

- **Online Appointment Surge:** Increased online patient bookings by 230% within 5 months.
- **Search Engine Leadership:** Achieved #1 Google ranking for key dental searches in Shahin Vila and Karaj with strategic [SEO services](/en/services/seo/).
- **Administrative Efficiency:** Dramatically reduced front-desk phone inquiries regarding insurance coverage.
