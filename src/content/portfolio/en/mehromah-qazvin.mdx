## 1. Project Overview

**Mehromah Commercial Complex** (mehromahqazvin.ir) is the cornerstone of modern retail in Qazvin, Iran. Spanning 5 commercial floors housing over 300 retail stores alongside a 7-story covered smart parking facility for 900+ vehicles, Mehromah needed a high-performance, modern digital platform to serve shoppers, store owners, and prospective commercial tenants.

## 2. The Challenge

- **Complex Real Estate & Directory Information:** Showcasing over 300 retail tenants across multiple floors while providing clear floor maps, operating hours, and parking availability updates.
- **Speed & Mobile Accessibility:** Mobile visitors require instant page load times when searching for directions, shop directories, or customer support while on the go.
- **Hyper-Local SEO Dominance:** Capitalizing on local shopping queries in Qazvin like _"Qazvin shopping center"_, _"Khayyam street parking"_, and _"Mehromah complex stores"_.
- **PWA & Interactive Experience:** Delivering an app-like mobile experience without requiring native app downloads.

## 3. The Technical Solution

### Astro Static Site Generation & Lightning Performance

- **Modern Astro & Tailwind Stack:** Built using Astro's island architecture and View Transitions API, achieving zero-JS baseline rendering and instant page transitions.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA) Capabilities:** Enabled Web App Manifest support and offline caching, allowing visitors to save the directory directly to their home screens.

### Local Business & ShoppingCenter Schema Integration

- **Rich Snippets (JSON-LD):** Implemented schema.org `ShoppingCenter`, `GeoCoordinates`, `OpeningHoursSpecification`, and `PostalAddress` data, embedding exact GPS coordinates to dominate Google Maps and Knowledge Panels.
- **Interactive Tenant Directory:** Designed an intuitive, searchable directory categorized by floor and business type (fashion, electronics, jewelry, food court).

### UX Aesthetics & Dark Mode Integration

- **Premium Visual Identity:** Crafted a modern, high-contrast interface featuring dynamic animations, high-resolution photography, and smooth scroll navigation.

## 4. Measuring the Results

- **Unrivaled Performance:** Achieved 98/100 Mobile PageSpeed score on Google Insights.
- **Local Search Leadership:** Ranked #1 on Google and Google Maps for major commercial queries in Qazvin.
- **Tenant Inquiry Boost:** Increased tenant leasing inquiries by 140% via the digital contact portal.
