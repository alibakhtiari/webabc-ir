## 1. Project Overview

**Mahsun Visa** (mahsunvisa.com) is an established immigration and educational advisory agency guiding applicants through study visas, work permits, investment migration, and tourist visa applications for destinations such as Canada, Europe, Australia, and the UK. With the motto *"From the darkness of doubts to the light of opportunities"*, Mahsun Visa needed a high-trust digital portal to educate applicants and capture qualified consultation leads.

## 2. The Challenge

*   **Establishing High Digital Authority & Trust:** Immigration is a high-stakes decision. The portal needed an ultra-clean corporate identity, transparent visa pathways, and verifiable success records.
*   **Interactive Visa Assessment & Booking:** Building an easy multi-step online assessment tool for applicants to evaluate visa eligibility prior to booking paid consultations.
*   **Information Architecture for Complex Regulations:** Structuring hundreds of migration guides (student permits, skilled worker points, investment programs) into an intuitive, easily searchable knowledge hub.

## 3. The Technical Solution

### Astra Pro Architecture & Custom Typography
*   **Custom Vazirmatn Typography Engine:** Implemented preloaded, optimized Vazirmatn font rendering for crystal-clear Persian typography and legibility across all screen resolutions.
*   **Interactive Consultation Funnel:** Built a dynamic multi-step assessment form syncing applicant profiles directly with CRM lead queues for immigration advisors.

### Performance & Technical SEO
*   **LiteSpeed Server Optimization:** Compressed all imagery and cached complex pages to ensure instant initial paint under 1.4 seconds.
*   **Structured Schema Markup:** Implemented JSON-LD `EducationalOrganization` and `FAQPage` schemas to dominate organic search results for visa queries.

## 4. Measuring the Results

*   **Lead Generation Surge:** Increased qualified migration consultation requests by 320%.
*   **Eligibility Tool Adoption:** Over 10,000 applicants completed online visa eligibility assessments.
*   **Search Engine Leadership:** Achieved top 3 Google rankings for key study visa and immigration search terms.
