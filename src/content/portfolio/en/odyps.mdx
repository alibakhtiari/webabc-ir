## 1. Project Overview

**ODYPS** (odyps.com) is a Scandinavian-based online retailer specializing in premium, high-density authentic Persian carpets and rugs (Persiska Mattor). Targeting design-conscious homeowners and interior architects across Sweden and Europe, ODYPS needed an elegant, high-performance e-commerce platform capable of showcasing intricate carpet craftsmanship while delivering a frictionless purchasing experience across multiple currencies and languages.

## 2. The Challenge

Expanding a luxury heritage product into competitive European markets presented several complex technical and marketing hurdles:

- **Multi-Currency & Multi-Lingual Friction:** Customers in Sweden prefer SEK with local Klarna-style payment expectations, while broader European buyers require EUR. The site needed seamless automatic currency conversion and localized content switching without SEO duplicate content penalties.
- **Heavy Visual Assets vs. Speed:** Luxury carpets require high-resolution imagery so customers can inspect weave density (700, 1200, and 1500 Reeds) and intricate pattern details. Unoptimized imagery was severely degrading page load times.
- **Complex Product Taxonomy:** Rugs vary by reed density, color tones (Marine Blue, Sand, Cream, Vintage), size, and material. Standard WooCommerce category navigation was inadequate for high-intent product filtering.
- **International SEO Competition:** Competing for high-value Nordic search queries like _"Persiska mattor online"_ required hyper-targeted localized technical SEO and structured product schemas.

## 3. The Technical Solution

### Customized WooCommerce Architecture & Multi-Currency Engine

- **Aelia Currency Switcher & Polylang Integration:** Built a unified multi-currency system allowing real-time currency switching between SEK and EUR based on geolocation and customer selection, with clean hreflang tagging to prevent indexation conflicts.
- **Custom Product Taxonomy & Attribute Filtering:** Developed an intuitive facet filter enabling buyers to instantly narrow down rugs by reed density (700, 1200, 1500 Reed), color combinations, and design collections (Diamond, Gold, Vintage).

### Next-Gen Image Optimization & Core Web Vitals

- **Next-Gen WebP Conversion & Dynamic Lazy-Loading:** Implemented automated WebP image optimization and responsive srcset attributes, allowing ultra-high-definition zoom capabilities while cutting asset payload sizes by 74%.
- **Caching & Asset Minification:** Engineered a custom caching layer on top of Cloudflare and Redis to deliver sub-1.2 second LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) across European servers.

### International SEO & Schema Markup

- **Localized Meta Strategy:** Formulated targeted SEO title structures and meta descriptions customized for Swedish and English search behaviors.
- **Rich Snippets (JSON-LD):** Implemented schema.org `Product`, `Offer`, `AggregateRating`, and `BreadcrumbList` markup, enabling Google rich snippets with prices, stock availability, and ratings directly in search results.

## 4. Measuring the Results

- **Conversion Rate Boost:** Frictionless multi-currency checkout reduced cart abandonment by 38%.
- **PageSpeed Excellence:** Achieved 96+ scores on Google PageSpeed Insights on desktop and mobile.
- **SEO Dominance:** Ranked on Page 1 in Sweden for critical high-volume keywords related to Persian rugs and high-reed carpets.
- **Expanded European Footprint:** Successfully scaled sales across Scandinavia and continental Europe.
