## 1. Project Overview

**Remido** (remido.ir) is Iran’s pioneer professional manufacturer of Kalimbas and artisan acoustic instruments (Lyres, Jaw Harps, Chimes, and Wind Instruments). Adapting world instruments for Persian musical scales and accessible playing, Remido needed an e-commerce platform that could combine direct-to-consumer sales, interactive audio previews, authorized dealer registration, and serial-based warranty validation.

## 2. The Challenge

- **Counterfeit & Market Protection:** As a leading innovator, Remido faced market counterfeits. They required a public serial-number validation system to guarantee customer authenticity.
- **Audio Preview Experience:** Customers needed to listen to clean, high-fidelity sound samples of different wood types (Mahogany, Walnut, Crystal) and tines before purchasing.
- **Dealer & Warranty Logistics:** Managing warranty activation via SMS and serial registration for thousands of instruments sold through national retail networks.
- **High-Volume E-Commerce Conversion:** Delivering a fast, mobile-friendly shopping experience with instant OTP registration via phone number.

## 3. The Technical Solution

### Customized E-Commerce & Warranty Verification Engine

- **Serial Number & Warranty Activation Portal:** Built a custom database integration where buyers enter their unique instrument serial number to verify authenticity, register 12-month warranties, and receive automated confirmation SMS.
- **Interactive Audio Sample Player:** Implemented lightweight HTML5 audio players on product pages allowing buyers to sample live acoustic tunings.

### High-Performance Flatsome Architecture

- **Custom WooCommerce Storefront:** Customized Flatsome theme architecture paired with LiteSpeed caching, optimizing mobile navigation and category filtering across Classic, Square, and Custom series.
- **OTP & SMS Integration:** Integrated Digits OTP login alongside IPPanel SMS gateway for frictionless user registration without requiring emails.

### Search Engine Dominance & Schema Markup

- **Category Niche Domination:** Optimized content architecture for high-intent queries like _"buy kalimba"_, _"professional kalimba price"_, and _"iranian handcrafted kalimba"_.
- **MusicStore & Product Schema:** Embedded custom Schema.org JSON-LD definitions to present product ratings, serial verification features, and brand details in Google SERPs.

## 4. Measuring the Results

- **Market Dominance:** Solidified Remido as the #1 search result and manufacturer for Kalimbas in Iran.
- **Authenticity Trust:** Over 15,000 instrument serials successfully registered in the warranty database.
- **Direct Sales Surge:** Boosted direct e-commerce sales by 340% while empowering nationwide retail partners.
