## 1. Project Overview

**Mapfan** (mapfan.ir) — _Mabna Pajoohan Fanavarihaye Novin Atiyeh_ — is an engineering enterprise delivering technology-driven solutions to upgrade manufacturing quality, industrial automation, and service efficiency across major industrial sectors. They needed a sophisticated corporate web portal that reflects their technical prowess, showcases complex engineering services, and drives high-value B2B inquiries.

## 2. The Challenge

- **Communicating Complex Technical Capabilities:** Presenting high-tech industrial automation and R&D engineering concepts in a clear, compelling manner for both technical directors and executive decision-makers.
- **Legacy Design & Low Engagement:** The previous digital presence failed to showcase the company's innovation footprint, leading to high bounce rates and low lead conversion.
- **B2B Industrial SEO:** Ranking for specialized industrial engineering search terms required a targeted technical SEO strategy and structured content taxonomy.

## 3. The Technical Solution

### Industrial UX Design & Custom Layouts

- **Modern Corporate Aesthetic:** Crafted a clean, high-contrast industrial interface emphasizing engineering precision, featuring dynamic service matrices, project showcases, and interactive infographics.
- **Modular Architecture:** Built custom Elementor Pro template structures with responsive fluid grids to ensure seamless viewing across desktop workstations, tablets, and mobile devices.

### Speed & Core Web Vitals Optimization

- **LiteSpeed Enterprise Caching:** Integrated LiteSpeed cache with critical CSS generation and lazy-loaded media assets to deliver sub-1.5 second page load times across corporate networks.
- **Code Optimization:** Cleaned up legacy script dependencies and minified inline CSS and JavaScript payloads.

### B2B SEO Strategy & Schema Integration

- **Targeted Keyword Architecture:** Mapped technical service offerings to high-intent industrial queries (automation solutions, production quality enhancement, smart engineering).
- **Organization & Service Schemas:** Embedded JSON-LD structured data for B2B organization profiles and service offerings to boost search engine visibility and rich snippet representation.

## 4. Measuring the Results

- **Lead Generation Growth:** Qualified B2B project inquiries increased by 160% within 4 months of launch.
- **Enhanced Brand Perception:** Elevated client trust during corporate procurement evaluations.
- **Search Engine Performance:** Ranked on Page 1 of Google for targeted industrial technology search queries.
